About the Workshop

Data4VFM brings together researchers working on data-centric approaches for building and improving visual and multimodal foundation models. As model architectures converge, the quality, curation, and design of training data has become one of the central levers for progress in computer vision and multimodal learning. This half-day workshop will explore topics including data curation and filtering, synthetic and augmented data generation, dataset bias and evaluation, and scaling laws for data.

We invite researchers and practitioners to share their work through paper submissions, and will host invited talks and a poster session bringing together perspectives from academia and industry.

Topics of Interest

We welcome submissions across the following areas (non-exhaustive).

Topic 1

Data Curation & Filtering

Methods for selecting, cleaning, and filtering large-scale visual and multimodal training data.

Topic 2

Synthetic & Augmented Data

Generative and simulation-based approaches for creating training data for foundation models.

Topic 3

Dataset Bias & Evaluation

Understanding, measuring, and mitigating bias in datasets used to train and evaluate VFMs.

Topic 4

Scaling Laws for Data

Empirical and theoretical studies of how data quantity and quality drive model performance at scale.

Submission Guidelines

We welcome submissions related to data curation, synthetic data, dataset bias, and scaling laws for visual and multimodal foundation models. Accepted papers will be presented as spotlight talks and posters. We will select a Best Paper Award.
