About the Workshop

The next generation of visual and multimodal foundation models will be shaped not only by scale, but by the quality, composition, alignment, and evolution of their data. Significant challenges remain in ethically constructing high-quality datasets, assessing data quality, aligning information across modalities, and continually refining data as models and deployment requirements evolve.

Data4VFM brings together researchers and practitioners developing data-centric approaches for visual and multimodal foundation models — with a focus on ethical dataset construction, data quality assessment, multimodal alignment, continual dataset improvement, synthetic data generation, and dataset construction for efficient pre-training, post-training, alignment, and adaptation of these models.

Topics of Interest

We welcome submissions across the following areas (non-exhaustive).

Topic 1

Ethical Dataset Construction & Curation

Image/video dataset construction, auditing, benchmark design, data provenance, licensing, privacy, and governance.

Topic 2

Data Selection & Pruning

Coreset methods, subset selection, dataset distillation, filtering, deduplication, data valuation, efficient dataset scaling strategies.

Topic 3

Data-Centric Learning & Evaluation

Active learning, human-in-the-loop annotation, scaling laws, long-tail discovery, failure analysis, data attribution, robustness evaluation, alignment training.

Topic 4

Multimodal & Video Foundation Datasets

Image-text and video-text datasets, 3D and point cloud datasets, datasets for multimodal pre-training/post-training/alignment, temporal data curation, cross-modal supervision, video-language learning.

Topic 5

Synthetic & Simulated Data

Diffusion-generated images and videos, simulation environments, data augmentation, synthetic data quality assessment, hybrid real-synthetic datasets.

Topic 6

Systems & Industrial Practice

Dataset exploration tools, large-scale data pipelines, human-in-the-loop workflows, production deployments, autonomous driving use cases, and industrial case studies.

Call for Papers

Accepted papers will be presented as posters, with select papers invited for contributed talks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the page limit?

The main paper is limited to 4 pages. References and appendices may be any length and do not count toward the 4-page limit.

Will accepted papers be published in the proceedings?

Accepted papers are non-archival and will not be published in the WACV proceedings.

Can I include an appendix?

Yes. Appendices can be included in the same PDF as the main paper and may contain any number of pages.

What kinds of papers are welcome?

We welcome original research, work in progress, and industrial case studies related to data-centric learning for visual and multimodal foundation models.

Is the review process double-blind?

Yes. Submissions must follow WACV's double-blind review guidelines and use the official WACV 2027 template.

How will accepted papers be presented?

Accepted papers will be presented as posters, with select papers invited for contributed talks.
